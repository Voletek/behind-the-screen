## Architecture

The repository layout (canonical `core/`, thin Kiro agents, deterministic tools, evidence tiers,
examples vs workspace) follows [songwriting-kb](https://github.com/Voletek/songwriting-kb), including
the contributions credited there (single source of truth and platform-neutral method, from Brian
Smith Clark's review; clean workspace folders, from 21machines' feedback).
