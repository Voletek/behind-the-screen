# SOP 06: Designing an NPC

> Create a walk-on, recurring sketch, or pillar voice sheet.
>
> **Canonical method:** `core/methodology/npc-voice.md`. This SOP is the step list; if they diverge, `core/` wins.
> **Agent:** npc-designer · **Time:** 5-20 min

---

## Prerequisites

- Bible tone and canon; existing NPC sheets for contrast

## Procedure

1. Choose the tier.
2. Want (concrete, active), need/wound, secret (link to a clue).
3. Won't cross / will cross under pressure.
4. Diction and one verbal habit (`references/NPC_VOICE_REFERENCE.md`); contrast with scene-mates.
5. Presence: one physical habit and a sensory signature.
6. Relationships and arc options.
7. Save `npcs/<id>.md`; add to state.json.

## Done When

- [ ] Quality gates in npc-voice.md pass
