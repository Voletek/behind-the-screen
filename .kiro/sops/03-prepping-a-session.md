# SOP 03: Prepping a Session

> Write the 10-section Session Brief for the next session.
>
> **Canonical method:** `core/methodology/session-prep.md`. This SOP is the step list; if they diverge, `core/` wins.
> **Agent:** session-planner · **Time:** 15-45 min

---

## Prerequisites

- Bible, state.json, last session log and review

## Procedure

1. Characters: goals, resources, spotlight from last time.
2. Strong start tied to an open thread.
3. 3-6 potential scenes (situations, not a sequence).
4. 10 secrets and clues; 3+ clues per required conclusion.
5. 2-4 fantastic locations.
6. 2-5 NPCs (want, voice line, knowledge, under threat).
7. Threats and encounters with exits (5e: `tools/encounter.py`).
8. Advance off-screen clocks; write them to state.json.
9. Rewards tied to PC goals.
10. Safety and tone check. Save `prep/session-NN.prep.md`.

## Done When

- [ ] All quality gates in session-prep.md pass
