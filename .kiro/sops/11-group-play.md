# SOP 11: Group Play in One Chat

> Run a game for several players sharing a chat or Discord channel.
>
> **Canonical method:** `core/methodology/running-the-game.md` → Solo vs Group. This SOP is the step list; if they diverge, `core/` wins.
> **Agent:** game-master · **Time:** —

---

## Prerequisites

- Session zero agreed the posting format and absent-player rule

## Procedure

1. Players post as `**Name (Character):** action`; `OOC:` for table talk.
2. GM addresses one named player per prompt.
3. Shared moments: collect declarations ("Got Lyra and Kael. Elara, you too?"), then resolve together.
4. Track spotlight per player in the session log; aim the next scene at the lowest.
5. Split party: cut every 1-2 exchanges, end each cut on a hook.
6. Secrets: private channel if available; otherwise vague public answers.
7. Any player's `X` applies to the whole table.
8. Absent players: their PCs follow the agreed rule.

- [ ] `## Spotlight` recorded in the session log
