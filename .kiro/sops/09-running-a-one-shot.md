# SOP 09: Running a One-Shot

> Prep and run a complete adventure in one session.
>
> **Canonical method:** `core/methodology/session-prep.md` → One-Shot Prep; `running-the-game.md`. This SOP is the step list; if they diverge, `core/` wins.
> **Agent:** session-planner → game-master · **Time:** Prep 30-60 min; play 2-4 hrs

---

## Prerequisites

- System chosen; player count known

## Procedure

1. Pitch in one sentence; expected length.
2. Pregenerated characters with links and reasons to be there, plus the option for players to build quick custom characters (SOP 13, fast mode) or tweak the pregens.
3. Short safety check (session-zero.md → Short Version).
4. Strong start; 5-8 scenes as situations; 10 clues.
5. One guaranteed climax reachable from anywhere; 2-3 endings.
6. Clocks that force the climax if play wanders.
7. Write `oneshots/<id>/ONESHOT.md` and `state.json` (see `examples/oneshots/`); validate.
8. Run it with SOP 02; cut to the climax with 45 minutes left.

## Done When

- [ ] The session reached an ending
