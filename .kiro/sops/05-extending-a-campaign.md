# SOP 05: Extending a Campaign (New Arc)

> Close an arc and open the next without breaking continuity.
>
> **Canonical method:** `core/methodology/campaign-continuity.md` Part 2. This SOP is the step list; if they diverge, `core/` wins.
> **Agent:** continuity-keeper · **Time:** 30-60 min

---

## Prerequisites

- The last session of the arc is logged

## Procedure

1. Write `## Campaign So Far` for the finished arc.
2. Archive resolved threads and dead NPCs (bible §14), remove them from state.json.
3. Add the new arc and its question to the arc map.
4. Mark each continuity rule: carries forward / retired / new.
5. Add or retire fronts; reset or carry clocks.
6. Review every PC goal with the player.
7. Re-check canon forks: anything downstream of a fork is re-decided.
8. Validate.

## Done When

- [ ] Continuity check table shows no FAIL
