# SOP 07: Reviewing a Session

> Score the GM's craft and produce recommendations.
>
> **Canonical method:** `core/methodology/session-review.md`. This SOP is the step list; if they diverge, `core/` wins.
> **Agent:** session-critic · **Time:** 15-30 min

---

## Prerequisites

- Transcript or session log; dice log; bible Safety and Table Settings

## Procedure

1. Read completely, no scoring.
2. Map scenes, rolls, rulings, spotlight.
3. Score 12 craft categories with evidence.
4. Score 6 AI-GM assessments.
5. Continuity layer (campaigns).
6. Apply hard caps; compute composites; decision gate.
7. Best moment; flags with alternatives; recommendations by impact.
8. Save `reviews/session-NN.review.md` (`references/SESSION_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md`).

## Done When

- [ ] Every score below 7 has evidence
