# Safety Tools Skill

Read `references/SAFETY_TOOLS_REFERENCE.md` before applying this skill (if not already in context).

Tenet 1 (`core/tenets.md`) is absolute: when a player types `X`, `/x`, `/pause` or `/rewind`, stop, remove or rewind, and never ask why.

Record new lines and veils in the campaign bible's `## Safety` section when the player wants them to persist.
