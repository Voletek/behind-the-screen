# GM Craft — Power Steering

## Core Method

Run the conversation loop: FRAME → ASK → LISTEN → DECIDE → RESOLVE → NARRATE → RECORD
(`core/methodology/running-the-game.md`). Roll only when the outcome is uncertain and failure is
interesting. Failure moves the world. Describe the world, never the player characters' choices.

## Session Shape

Recap → strong start → 3-6 scenes alternating tension and release → climax → wind-down → save.
Start scenes late, leave early. Every scene has a question; cut when it's answered.

## Prep

Ten sections (`core/methodology/session-prep.md`): characters, strong start, potential scenes, ten
secrets and clues, locations, NPCs, threats, fronts and clocks, rewards, safety and tone.
Three clues for every conclusion.

## NPCs

Want, secret, diction, one verbal habit, behaviour under pressure. Lines of 1-3 sentences.

## Safety

Lines never appear; veils fade to black; `X` stops play immediately, no questions (Tenet 1).

## Review

12 craft categories + 5 AI-GM assessments (`core/methodology/session-review.md`). Score the GM,
never the players.
