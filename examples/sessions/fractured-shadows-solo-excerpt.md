# Example Transcript — Fractured Shadows, Session 1 (solo excerpt)
<!-- validate: skip -->

> Shows the conversation loop, honest dice lines, a canon fork, the Strain house rule, and the
> name-revelation rule in a solo game. GM = the AI. System: `rules-light`. Matching files:
> `examples/campaigns/fractured-shadows/` (session log, dice log, state).

---

**GM:** Cold first. Wet stone under your cheek, a slow drip somewhere to your left, and a smell of rot
with metal underneath it. You are small, smaller than you should be, and the only light in the
dark is coming from your own chest: three interwoven triangles, glowing in time with your heartbeat.
Somewhere ahead, something scrapes on rock and stops.

What do you do?

**Player:** I try to talk. Say "hello?" quietly.

**GM:** It comes out as a squeak, high and reedy, nothing like your voice. The scraping starts again,
closer, and now there are three sounds: two light, one dragging.

**Player:** OOC: I know this place. I designed the Whispering Caves. Can I use "I coded this" to know
the layout?

**GM:** OOC: Yes, that's your Developer's memory tag, once per session. You get an edge on finding a
way through. The cost: I tell you one way the real place differs from your design.

You know the river route. It's where you put it. But the cave is bigger than your map: the ceiling
is lost in the dark, and there are carvings on the walls you never made.

**Player:** I press myself into the rock and wait for them to come to me. When they pass, I go for the
one dragging the spear.

**GM:** Three goblins, larger than you remember making them, red eyes catching the Mark's glow. The one
with the broken spear limps past your crevice. You're in darkness; that's an edge. Grace.

🎲 Rikan, strike from the shadows: 3d6kh2+2 → [6, (2), 5] + 2 = 13 ✔✔ critical

Your claws find the back of its knee and it drops, shrieking. The other two spin toward the sound.
Your heart is very loud in your ears.

*(…the fight continues; one goblin is killed, the third breaks and runs…)*

**GM:** The last goblin bolts for the tunnel mouth. You're faster.

🎲 Rikan, cut it off: pbta+2 → [4, 6] + 2 = 12 ✔✔ critical

You're in front of it before it knows you've moved. It freezes, shaking, blade dropped. It's small up
close. It smells of peat. You have it.

**Player:** …I let it go. I step aside.

**GM:** It stares at you for one breath, then it's gone into the dark. Later, if you ask the walls, they
might tell you its name.

**Player:** OOC: What's his name?

**GM:** OOC: Good question. Let's say Skrit. I'll note it. *(Canon fork logged: the book has Rikan kill
all three.)*

Under the dead leader, half-buried in debris, something is glowing. An obsidian shard, the size of your
palm, pulsing in the same rhythm as the Mark. When your fingers close on it, the pulse jumps into your
arm and your chest answers. [Strain +1 → 1/6]

**Player:** I check it over. Am I "the chosen one" now?

**GM:** Nothing tells you that. What you feel is weight: every choice in front of you seems closer and
heavier, as if the shard is leaning on it.

---

## What to notice

- **No puppeting:** the GM never says what Rikan feels about the kill; the player decides to spare Skrit.
- **Honest dice:** every roll is a tool result line, logged in `dice-log.jsonl`.
- **Fork, not railroad:** the book's outcome (kill all three) became a choice; the fork is in the bible.
- **Canon rules:** no prophecy presented as true fate (Rule 6); the name "Draeven" is never used (Rule 1);
  the Shard costs Strain (house rule).
- **Short GM turns** that end on something to act on.
