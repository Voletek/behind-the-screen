# Example Transcript — The Ferryman of Hundred Reeds (group excerpt)

> Shows group-chat conventions: named prompts, `**Name (Character):**` posts, collecting simultaneous

**GM:** Dawn mist on the river. On the near bank a Marquisate cart is sunk to its axles, and its cat
captain, Mirelle, is shouting at thirty denizens who want to cross. On the far bank, Gristle's raft
bobs against a post with a single blue feather nailed to it. Someone in the crowd yells, "Ask the
vagabonds, they're paid to care!" Every head turns to you.

Tansy, you're closest to the captain. What do you do?

**Kira (Tansy):** I hop up on the cart's wheel so everyone can see me and tell them nobody crosses
until we know what happened to Gristle.

**Marcus (Coin):** While everyone's looking at Tansy I slip toward the cart. Supply carts have
strongboxes.

**GM:** Got Tansy and Coin. Brother Wren, you too, or watching?

**Sam (Brother Wren):** Watching the crowd. If it turns ugly I step between the cats and the denizens.

**GM:** Tansy first. You're trying to calm a crowd that's scared and angry: that's Persuade an NPC,
the crowd as one NPC.

🎲 Tansy, roll Persuade: 2d6 + Charm (+0) · at stake: the crowd turns on someone — roll and post the result.

**Kira (Tansy):** 🎲 5 and 3, so 8.

**GM:** 8, a partial success. They quiet down, but they'll want something for waiting: a promise, or someone to blame. Your call.
*(A 7-9 cost offered in the spirit of the move; the book has the move's exact options.)*

**Kira (Tansy):** A promise. "Ferry runs by dusk or I swim you across myself."

**GM:** Noted. That's a promise with your name on it. *(Thread opened: Tansy's dusk promise.)*

Coin, nobody's watching you. The strongbox is chained under the driver's seat, and the lock is good
Marquisate steel.

**Marcus (Coin):** Pick it.

**GM:** 🎲 Coin, roll Pick Lock (roguish feat): 2d6 + Finesse (+2) · at stake: you're seen with a paw in a
Marquisate cart, in front of thirty witnesses. Roll when you're ready, or change your mind.

**Marcus (Coin):** In. 🎲 1d6+1d6+2 → [1, 2] + 2 = 5 (rolled with roll.py)

**GM:** 5, a miss. The lock gives, and so does the seat. It drops with a bang that turns every head, Mirelle's first.
She's staring at you, and at the open box full of orders sealed with a red wax paw.

Brother Wren, two of the captain's soldiers are already moving toward Coin, hands on hilts. The crowd
is between you and them. What do you do?

**Sam (Brother Wren):** X

**GM:** Got it, removing that. *(Rewinding the soldiers.)* Mirelle is staring at Coin and the open box,
and she hasn't called anyone yet. She's thinking. Brother Wren, what do you do?

**Sam (Brother Wren):** OOC: thanks. I walk over and stand next to Coin, calm, and say the Arbiter
keeps the peace here, so any dispute goes through me.

## What to notice

- **Named prompts:** every GM turn ends with a named player.
- **Collecting declarations:** "Got Tansy and Coin. Brother Wren, you too?"
- **Players roll their own dice:** the GM sends a roll request, stops, and waits for Kira's and
  Marcus's results (physical dice and `roll.py` are both fine).
- **Stakes before the roll** for Coin's risky move, from what Coin can see, with room to back out.
- **The miss moves the world:** Coin is caught and finds a clue (sealed orders), not "nothing happens".
- **X-card:** no question, no explanation asked, the content is removed and play continues.
- **Spotlight rotation:** Tansy → Coin → Wren, with the quietest player brought in next.
