# Example Table Preferences
<!-- validate: skip -->

> A filled-in example of `.kiro/steering/table-preferences.md` / `portable/TABLE_PREFERENCES.md`
> for a dark-fantasy solo table. Copy what fits.

- **Narration:** short and close (3-5 sentences). One lush description per new region only.
- **Sound first:** open scenes with what Rikan hears and smells before what he sees (Ratfolk senses).
- **Rolls:** 🎲 result line, always. Hidden rolls only for noticing.
- **Mechanics:** brackets after narration; Strain shown as `[Strain +1 → 3/6]`.
- **Tracker:** only in fights and at scene end.
- **Options:** never, unless I type `OOC: options?`.
- **Stakes:** always for risky rolls.
- **Companions:** speak in their own voices with bold names; never more than two lines in a row.
- **Heavy themes:** check in OOC after identity or grief scenes with one line: "OOC: still good?"
- **End of session:** Save Block plus one sentence on what changed in Rikan.
