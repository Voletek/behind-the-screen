# The Thaw at Pinebrook — One-Shot

> (the book is the authority).

## Pitch
The snowmelt has come early. Pinebrook, a mill village on the stream, sent a runner: the stream is
rising, the mill wheel has jammed, and the miller's apprentice went to clear it and didn't come back.
The patrol must reach Pinebrook, save who can be saved, and get the mill turning before the flood.

## Safety
- **Tone:** pastoral peril. Earnest, small heroes, real danger from water and predators.
- **Veils:** drowning described past one sentence; predators eating mice on screen.
- **In-play tool:** `X` alone on a line.

## Strong Start
The patrol is on the high path above the stream when the ice dam upstream cracks like a falling tree.
Meltwater floods the path behind them. Ahead, the bridge to Pinebrook groans; one plank already floats
away. A mouse on the far bank is waving a red scarf: the village's alarm.

## Mission and GM's Turn Obstacles
**Mission:** reach Pinebrook and restore the mill before the flood takes the granary.
1. **Weather / wilderness:** crossing the failing bridge in meltwater. Pathfinder or Weather Watcher test vs
   **Ob 3**. Twist on failure: Fennick (or another mouse) goes into the water (Swimmer Ob 3 to rescue), or
   the patrol takes the long way (Tired).
2. **Animals:** a hungry **heron** stalks the shallows by the mill race (an extended conflict: fight or
   chase; the heron's Nature is high and its goal is "eat one mouse"). Clever plans (fishing nets,
   distracting with a decoy) should change the conflict type.
3. **Mice (optional twist):** the miller blames the patrol's lateness and refuses help; Persuader vs
   the miller's Will.

## Scenes
1. **The bridge** · *Will they all get across?*
2. **Pinebrook in panic** · *Who leads the villagers?*
3. **The mill race** · the apprentice is clinging to the jammed wheel · *Can they reach him past the heron?*
4. **Unjamming the wheel** · Carpenter Ob 4 with help; the wheel turning drains the race · *Will the granary flood?*
5. **Players' turn** · recover, pursue goals, prepare for the trip home.

## NPCs
- **Miller Hask:** proud, frightened, loud. Wants his mill saved more than he admits he wants his apprentice saved.
- **Pip the apprentice:** soaked, brave, holding a wrench he won't let go of.
- **The heron:** patient, silent, terrible.

## Clocks
- `flood` (6): ticks on each failed test and each scene of delay. At 6 the granary floods (the village
  faces a hungry spring: a lasting consequence for the next mission).

## Endings
- **Saved:** the wheel turns, the race drains, Pip is home. Pinebrook owes the Guard.
- **Saved at a cost:** the mill turns but the granary floods; the Guard must bring grain before summer.
- **Lost:** Pip is taken by the heron or the flood. The patrol carries the news to Lockhaven.
