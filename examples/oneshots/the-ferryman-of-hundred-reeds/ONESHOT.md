## Pitch
Hundred Reeds is a marsh clearing where the only way across the great river is old Gristle's ferry.
The Marquisate wants the crossing for its supply carts. The Eyrie wants it closed. The Woodland
Alliance smuggles its people across at night. This morning, Gristle didn't open the ferry, and his
raft is tied up on the wrong bank. Someone is paying vagabonds to find out why.

## Strong Start
Dawn mist on the river. On the near bank, a Marquisate supply cart is stuck in the mud and its cat
captain is shouting at a crowd of denizens who want to cross. On the far bank, Gristle's raft bobs,
tied to a post that has a single blue Eyrie feather nailed to it. Somebody in the crowd yells "Ask the
vagabonds, they're paid to care!"

## The Clearing
- **Controlled by:** contested. The Marquisate holds the road; the Eyrie holds the sky; the Alliance
  holds the reeds.
- **What's broken:** the crossing. Without it, the clearing starves within a week.
- **Who wants the vagabonds:** Captain Mirelle (Marquisate) wants the ferry running; Wing-Lord
  Aster's envoy (Eyrie) wants it closed; Old Nettle (Alliance) wants Gristle found alive.

6. **The crossing at dusk** · climax: every faction converges on the ferry · *Who controls the river tomorrow?*

## Secrets and Clues
1. Gristle closed the ferry himself: the Eyrie threatened his granddaughter.
2. The blue feather was planted by an Alliance agent to blame the Eyrie.
3. The toll box was taken by Gristle's granddaughter, Pim, to pay the Eyrie off.
4. Captain Mirelle is ordered to burn the ferry if she can't use it.
5. The Eyrie envoy secretly wants the Marquisate humiliated more than the ferry closed.
6. Old Nettle is using the crisis to move Alliance fighters across in disguise.
7. Gristle is hidden in the reed maze by the Alliance, "for his protection".
8. The winch has been sabotaged with a cut cable (Sedge can tell).

## NPCs
- **Gristle** (old muskrat ferryman): stubborn, kind, speaks in river sayings ("The current don't
  choose sides").
- **Captain Mirelle** (cat, Marquisate): crisp, tired, by-the-book.
- **Envoy Kestrel** (hawk, Eyrie): grand, formal, contemptuous of "ground-walkers".
- **Old Nettle** (hedgehog, Alliance): gentle voice, ruthless plans.
- **Pim** (muskrat kit): scared, clever, holding the toll box.

## Fronts and Clocks
- `ferry-burns` (6): the Marquisate's patience. At 6, Mirelle burns the ferry.
- `alliance-rising` (4): Nettle's fighters cross; at 4, an Alliance uprising starts in Hundred Reeds.

## Reputation
Every faction notices who the vagabonds helped. Record reputation changes in `state.json → factions`.

## Endings
- **The river runs free:** the ferry reopens under Gristle, owing no faction. Everyone is a little angry.
- **The river changes hands:** one faction holds the crossing. Reputation shifts hard.
- **The ferry burns:** the clearing faces a hungry week; the war gets closer.
