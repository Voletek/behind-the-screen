# Shell Game — One-Shot
<!-- oneshot: shell-game · system: tmnt · players: 2-4 · length: ~3 hours -->

> Original mutant-animal heroes in the gritty-comic tone of *TMNT & Other Strangeness*. Mechanics per
> `systems/tmnt/PROFILE.md` (the Palladium book is the authority). No canonical characters; add
> cameos only if the table wants them. No book? Run it on `rules-light` (see the profile's §9).

## Pitch
A crate of experimental mutagen went missing from a waterfront lab, and three street gangs think the
others have it. Somebody dumped a sample in the storm drains under Ninth Street, and the rats down
there are starting to talk. The heroes live in those drains. They have until morning before the gangs
tear the neighbourhood apart looking for it.

## Safety
- **Tone:** gritty comic (black-and-white, rain, neon), with brotherly humour.
- **Lines/veils:** no insanity or mental-illness tables from the 1985 printing (Tenet 1; the Redux
  removed them). Veil: harm to civilians beyond a sentence.
- **In-play tool:** `X` alone on a line.

## Strong Start
Midnight in the drains. A swarm of ordinary rats pours past the heroes' lair, every one of them
running *upstream*, away from Ninth Street. Behind them, something the size of a dog is singing the
jingle from a pizza commercial, badly, in a voice like gravel.

## Scenes
1. **The singing thing** · a rat mutated an hour ago, terrified and huge · *Can they calm it before it hurts someone?*
2. **The lab** · police tape, a dead camera, a gang tag on the loading door · *Who took the crate?*
3. **Three gangs, one block** · the Gulls (docks), the Neon Saints (club), the 9th Street Iron (garage) · *Who has it?*
4. **The drain junction** · the sample leaking into the water main · *Can they stop it reaching the city supply?*
5. **The trade** · the real thief sells the crate at dawn on the waterfront · *Who gets it?*

## Secrets and Clues
1. The tag on the door is a fake: wrong colour paint for the Gulls.
2. The night guard took the crate himself to stop the lab selling it to a weapons buyer.
3. The guard is hiding in the drains, mutating from a cracked vial.
4. The weapons buyer has hired the Neon Saints as muscle for the dawn trade.
5. The mutagen reverses in sunlight for an hour (Hex's hope, and a trap).
6. The 9th Street Iron's boss is Dozer's older sister.

## Threats
- Gang muscle: low-level humans, numbers (3-6), chains and bats, one with a pistol. Use the book's
  generic thug stats or level 1-2 street NPCs.
- **The mutated guard:** a big, confused mutant; high S.D.C.; wants his family to be safe.
- **The buyer's enforcer:** a trained fighter, level 4-5, with body armour.

## Clocks
- `gang-war` (6): ticks on each scene that ends with the gangs suspicious of each other. At 6, a street
  fight on Ninth Street with civilians in the way.
- `water-main` (4): at 4 the sample reaches the main.

## Endings
- **Clean:** the mutagen is destroyed or hidden; the guard is safe; no gang war.
- **Messy:** the crate is lost to the buyer; someone new is mutating somewhere in the city (sequel hook).
- **Bad:** the water main. The whole neighbourhood starts to change.
