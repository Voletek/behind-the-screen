# Session 02 Prep — Fractured Shadows
<!-- prep: session-02 · system: rules-light · date: 2026-11-14 -->

## 1. Characters
- **Rikan:** harm 1/4, strain 2/6, Shaken. Goal: out of the caves; what is the Shard? Touch the
  *who-am-i* thread at least once (Ratfolk instinct vs developer memory). Developer's memory refreshed.

## 2. Strong Start
The river rises in the night. Rikan wakes with water at his tail and the Shard pulsing hard enough
to light the chamber. Upstream, something large moves against the current. Downstream, the flooded
tunnel his Ratfolk instincts insist on. Behind him, the spider gallery his memory says is the way out.

## 3. Potential Scenes
- **Flooded tunnel** · river · drowning vs instinct · *Can he trust the body's knowledge?*
- **Spider gallery** · webs, egg sacs · being eaten · *Can he trust the developer's map?*
- **Ratfolk waystone** · an abandoned haven cache · kinship · *What did his people leave for whoever came next?*
- **The Mire's edge** · surface, first daylight, Fracture-glitched sky · *What does Eryndor really look like?*
- **Skrit returns** · Mire paths · a debt · *Mercy remembered or mercy sold?*
- **Refugees on the plains road** · first portent of the siege · *Will he stop to help?*

## 4. Secrets and Clues
1. Ratfolk havens leave caches marked with three scratches and a tail-curl.
2. The waystone cache holds a Ratfolk elder's route-song; humming it calms Fractured Beasts nearby.
3. The large thing upstream is a Fractured salamander drawn to the Shard, not hunting him.
4. Spiders avoid the Shard's light; they do not avoid the Mark's.
5. Skrit's tribe sells to "the cloaked ones" who pay for anything that glows (Covenant agents).
6. The glyph tablet's style matches the Custodian sigils at Dawnwatch's obsidian tower.
7. The Mark of Balance has been seen before, on a Custodian who did not survive it (Alaric's secret).
8. Refugees on the road speak of a Freehold called Dawnwatch, east, with a black tower.
9. The sky over the Mire flickers like a bad render (memory bleed; costs nothing, means something).
10. "Toward the light" on the tablet may mean Dawnwatch's beacons.

## 5. Fantastic Locations
- **The Flooded Tunnel:** cold, black, the roof one paw above the water; air pockets marked by moss;
  the current talks.
- **The Ratfolk Waystone:** a pillar of stacked flat stones in a dry side-cave; tail-curl marks;
  a cache that collapses safely if pulled wrong.
- **The Mire's Edge:** peat smell, will-o-wisps that are actually Fracture sparks, distant beacons.

## 6. NPCs
- **Skrit** (goblin): wants to sell the "glow-rat" to the cloaked ones *or* to repay the mercy (2d6 on
  meeting: 7+ repay). Talks fast, third person ("Skrit knows a path").
- **Hela Marsh-Ward** (Freehold refugee): wants to get her children to Dawnwatch. Plain, tired, calls
  everyone "cuz" once they share food.

## 7. Threats and Encounters
- **Fractured salamander** · river · wants the Shard's warmth · threat: Standard (3 harm) · exit: throw
  something warm, hum the route-song, or out-swim it.
- **Giant spiders (3)** · gallery · want food · Standard · exit: the Shard's light; burn the webs.
