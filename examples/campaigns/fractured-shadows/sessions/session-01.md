# Session 01 — The Cave Knows My Name
<!-- session: 01 · campaign: fractured-shadows · system: rules-light · mode: solo · date: 2026-11-14 -->

## Recap
- Rikan woke in the Whispering Caves beneath the Black Mire, in a Ratfolk body, the Mark of Balance
  glowing on his chest. His voice came out as a squeak; his instincts came out as a rogue's.
- Three goblins found him. He killed two from the shadows and **let the third (Skrit) flee**.
- Under the dead leader he found the **obsidian Shard**; it answered the Mark with a pulse.
- He followed the underground river to a sealed alcove and found a **Custodian warning-glyph tablet**,
  not a prophecy (canon fork, see bible §12).
- He is resting by the river, shaken, two segments from finding the way out.

## Scenes
1. **Awakening** — Can he get his bearings before something finds him? → Yes: he placed himself (the
   Whispering Caves; "I coded this", but the cave is bigger and older than his map).
2. **The First Encounter** — Will he survive three goblins? → Yes, at a cost: 1 harm, and the first
   kill. Spared the third.
3. **The Shard** — What is under the leader? → The Shard. 1 Strain on first touch.
4. **The River and the Alcove** — What is behind the water curtain? → Custodian glyphs: "Sealed.
   Do not carry it toward the light." 1 more Strain reading them with the Shard active.

## Rulings
- Spared goblin: on a 12 (critical) Grace roll to cut off the fleeing goblin, the player chose to let
  it go instead of killing it. Ruled that the success gave him the choice, and the goblin got a name (Skrit)
  because the player asked what he was called.
- Reading glyphs with the Shard: counted as using the Shard (house rule, Nexus Strain), 1 Strain.
