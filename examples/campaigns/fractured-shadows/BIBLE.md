# Fractured Shadows — Campaign Bible

> **System:** player's choice; this example runs on `rules-light` (with Strain), with conversion notes
> for `dnd-5e` · **Mode:** solo (Rikan's Road) or group (Survivors of the Plains) ·
> **Started:** 2026-11-14 · **Source canon:** *Fractured Shadows* (Book 1) and *Shattered Veil*
> (Book 2) by Voletek, the *Eryndor World Codex*, and the *Fractured Shadows* concept album
> (songwriting-kb `examples/albums/fractured-shadows/BLUEPRINT.md`).
>
> **Thesis:** "The fracture made the shadows — but the shadows made the dawn."
>
> Canon authority (from the Codex, §I.2): finalized book text → author approvals → the Codex →
> supporting notes. Conflicts are never silently reconciled: they are listed under
> **Open Canon Questions** for the author to rule on. At the table, **table canon** wins once play
> diverges (Tenet 6), and the fork is logged in §12.

## 1. Premise

A burned-out game developer wakes in the Whispering Caves beneath the Black Mire, inside the body of
the Ratfolk rogue he designed, Rikan Tunnelsprint. The world he built, Eryndor, is real, and it is not
the world he remembers coding. It is a damaged, self-regulating reality held together by the Nexus,
cracking into Fractures that spawn Riftborn and Fracture storms. On his chest glows the Mark of
Balance; in his paw, an obsidian Shard that makes every decision heavier. Factions want the Mark, the
Shard, or him. The question the campaign asks: **can someone with two names become whole in a world
that punishes every use of power?**

## 2. Tone

- **Tone line:** grim but hopeful fantasy (`references/GENRE_TONE_LIBRARY.md` → Grim / Dark Fantasy,
  70%, with Found Family warmth, 30%). Power always costs; people who stay are home.
- **Touchstones:** the *Fractured Shadows* album (Act 2: tribal → cinematic orchestral → folk);
  the Codex's "damaged engine governor" world; *The Witcher 3* for moral grey and weight.
- **Lethality:** real. Death needs a high-stakes scene flagged OOC first ("This could kill Rikan. Go
  ahead?"). **Difficulty feel:** fair but hard. **Humour:** gallows, and warm at campfires.
  **Romance:** fade-to-black if it arises.
- **Sensory palette by arc** (from the album): caves = drip, dark, fungus-glow, silence; plains = wind,
  cello-wide skies, warped distance; Dawnwatch = stone, bells, obsidian tower, forge smoke; war = noise,
  then birdsong after.

## 3. Safety

- **Lines:** defaults from `references/SAFETY_TOOLS_REFERENCE.md` (sexual violence, harm to children
  shown on screen, real-world hate speech, graphic torture of PCs).
- **Veils:** graphic gore; body horror beyond a sentence (Fracture mutations are described, not
  dwelt on); self-harm (Draeven's burnout is explored as exhaustion and isolation, not self-harm).
- **Themes in scope (opted in):** burnout, isolation, guilt, grief, identity dissociation, men's
  mental health. Handle with care: show, don't lecture; check in after heavy scenes.
- **In-play tool:** `X` alone on a line = remove it, no questions. `/pause`, `/rewind`, `/ff`.
- **Check-ins:** after Fracture/identity scenes and any death.

## 5. House Rules

| Rule | Adopted | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Nexus Strain.** Using magic, the Mark or the Shard marks Strain (rules-light: 1-3 boxes by scale). At 6, a lasting consequence: memory bleed, identity slip, mutation, or a local Fracture. Strain clears slowly (1 box per safe long rest, max). | Session 0 | Codex §IV.1: amplification, distortion, debt |
| **The Shard compresses.** When Rikan hesitates with the Shard active, the GM may force the decision now (Shard pulse). Rolls with the Shard active treat 7-9 as either full success **plus 1 Strain** or partial success (player's choice). | Session 0 | Codex §VI.4.4: "not empowered, compressed" |
| **Unreliable roads.** Travel through Fracture-touched land: roll 2d6 + Wits on arrival; 6- = arrive changed (Strain, lost time, or a memory blur). | Session 0 | Codex §II.6 |
| **Developer's memory.** Once per session, Rikan may declare "I coded this", and gain an edge, but the GM then reveals one way the real thing differs from his design. | Session 0 | Book 1 premise + Codex: the world is not the code |
| **dnd-5e conversion** (if the table picks 5e): keep spell slots, but every spell of 1st level or higher cast inside a Fracture zone, and every Shard use, requires a DC 10 + spell level CON save; failure = 1 Exhaustion level (the Codex's "fatigue, stress"). Rikan: Ratfolk (homebrew, Small, darkvision 60 ft, proficiency in Stealth) Rogue 1. | Optional | Bridges the Codex's deprecated spell-slot model with 5e expectations |

## 7. Setting Canon (Arc I needs)

Summarised from the Codex; read the Codex for depth.

- **The Nexus:** the stabilising architecture of reality. Not a god, not moral. Under strain it fails
  locally, producing **Fractures**: warped terrain, temporal echoes, sensory distortion, mutation.
- **Balance** is equilibrium, not good vs evil. The world does not bend to morality; people must.
- **Fractured Beasts / Riftborn:** damaged organisms, not evil. Erratic, drawn to Nexus-active beings
  (like Rikan).
- **The Mark of Balance:** three interwoven triangles glowing on Rikan's chest; it hums with Nexus
  energy and reacts to his emotions (Book 1).
- **The Shard:** an obsidian fragment of a larger, unknown construct tied to the Nexus. It amplifies
  intent and accelerates consequence. Costs: strain, memory bleed, emotional amplification, identity
  destabilisation, exhaustion.
- **Magic** is opening a valve in a pressurised system: easier in damaged regions, dangerous when
  optimised. No gods answer prayers.
- **Travel** is unreliable: distance shifts with emotion and Fracture proximity; no fast travel without
  severe cost.
- **Places (Arc I):**
  - **Whispering Caves** under the **Black Mire**, a swamp on the edge of the kingdom of **Eldoria**
    (Book 1). Phosphorescent moss, an underground river, glyph-carved walls, goblins, giant spiders.
  - **Ratfolk ancestral networks:** modular, redundant tunnels engineered to collapse safely.
  - **The Fractured Plains** (album T10): open land where distance lies.
  - **Freehold of Dawnwatch:** dwarven-reinforced walls, funnel streets, central black obsidian
    watchtower, signal beacons; humans and dwarves, few elves. Custodian-influenced, not ruled.
- **Cultures:** Ratfolk (silence, preparedness, kinship by deed; abandonment unforgivable); Freeholds
  ("bro/sis/cuz" = obligation); Shadowvine (three-layer speech; trust through shared risk).

## 8. Factions and Fronts

### Front: The Riftborn Tide — the Siege of Dawnwatch
- **Goal:** the Shadow Weaver drives Riftborn against Dawnwatch to break it and reach the Nexus beneath.
- **Clock:** `dawnwatch-siege`, 8 segments. **Ticks when:** PCs delay, Fracture storms are left
  unchecked, Dawnwatch's eastern gate stays unguarded, the Custodian is drawn away.
- **Portents:** 2 refugees on the plains road · 4 outer farms burn · 6 the eastern gate opened from
  inside · 8 Dawnwatch falls (album T12) **unless the PCs have changed the situation**.

### Front: The Crimson Legion
- **Goal:** General **Valerius** wants the Mark as a weapon to unite Eryndor under his rule (Book 1).
- **Clock:** `legion-hunt`, 6 segments. **Ticks when:** the Mark is used openly, rumours spread.
- **If unopposed:** Legion soldiers seize Rikan or someone he loves as leverage.

### Front: The Obsidian Covenant
- **Goal:** an ancient enemy whose motives stay a mystery for a long time (Book 1); **Malkor**, shadow
  sorcerer, second only to the hidden leader. Behind them, **"The Architect"** (Book 2): a collective
  of mages who have manipulated the Nexus for centuries.
- **Clock:** `covenant-shard`, 6 segments. **Ticks when:** the Shard is used; its pulse is a beacon.

### Front: Nexus Strain (world clock)
- **Clock:** `nexus-strain`, 8 segments, ticks between arcs and on catastrophic Shard use.
  **Portents:** stronger Fracture storms, Aurora Rivers glowing brighter, Custodian schisms going public.

### Other powers
- **Custodians:** maintainers, not rulers; cells; schisms (Containment Absolutists, Interventionists,
  Shard Monitors). Alaric Dawnwatcher is one.
- **Skyreach Consortium** (Porthaven trade, Aetherium extraction), **Order of the Verdant Bloom**
  (nature mages), **Duskwing Clan** (shadow magic, Veridia), **Shadowvine networks**, the **Sylvani**
  of the deep woods (Book 1-2).

## 11. Continuity Rules

1. The name **"Draeven Vole"** never appears in GM narration or NPC dialogue until an external
   source confirms it in play (Codex §I.8.2). The player may use it OOC.
2. **No gods answer.** No divine miracles or god-granted magic (Codex §I.10.1). Faith exists; it is not
   answered.
3. **No game UI in the fiction.** No stat windows, levels or "quest log" language in narration
   (Codex §I.10.2). Mechanics stay in brackets.
4. **Every use of magic, the Mark or the Shard has a cost:** Strain or a described consequence (Codex §IV.1).
5. **No fast travel** without a rare artifact and a severe consequence (Codex §II.6.4).
6. **No prophecy is presented as true fate.** Prophecy may exist as in-world belief (Open Canon Question 1).
7. **Custodians never govern.** They contain and they choose what cannot be saved (Codex §V.2).
8. **Retired names are never used** (see Retired Terms; checked by the validator).
9. **Species are not moral categories;** no alignment by race (Codex §VII.10.1).
10. **Alaric withholds; he does not lie.** Deep, measured, warm voice; same voice every appearance (album rule 2).
11. **The Shadow Weaver is never seen directly before Arc III:** only her effects and her whispered name (album T13).
12. **Draeven's old world appears only as memory bleed** in Arcs I-IV, never as a place the PCs can go
    (Open Canon Question 5).
