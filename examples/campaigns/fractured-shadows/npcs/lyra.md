# Lyra
<!-- npc: lyra · tier: pillar (solo-mode companion) · campaign: fractured-shadows -->

**Function:** companion; steadfast loyalty; the friend whose absence would be loud (album T12)
**Look:** Freehold archer, braid tied with bowstring, counts her arrows out loud
**Want:** a home that doesn't have to be defended every night
**Need / wound:** lost her first Freehold to a Fracture storm; doesn't let herself want things
**Secret** (table prep, not book canon): she joined the Dawnwatch patrols to look for her brother, missing on the plains road
**Won't cross:** abandon someone in her care (Freehold kinship: "cuz" means obligation)
**Will cross under pressure:** disobey Custodian orders

## Voice
- **Diction:** plain, quick, Freehold casual ("bro", "cuz" when she has decided you are hers).
- **Verbal habit:** counts things: arrows, steps, hours till dawn.
- **Sample lines:**
  - "Fourteen arrows. That's fourteen problems I can solve. You're the fifteenth."
  - "You don't get to call me sis yet. Earn it."
- **Under pressure:** gets funnier, then very quiet.

## Companion Rules (solo play)
- She suggests; Rikan decides (running-the-game.md → Solo companions).
- She can be wrong, and she can disagree loudly.
- She never lands the decisive blow in a climax.

## Open Canon Question
The album makes Lyra the archer; Book 1 calls her a young warrior. Table default: archer (bible Open
Canon Question 3). Her death in canon is a **front**, not a fixed event.
