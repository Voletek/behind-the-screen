# Alaric Dawnwatcher
<!-- npc: alaric · tier: pillar · campaign: fractured-shadows -->

**Function:** mentor; the weight of trust (album T11 "The Custodian")
**Look:** tall, grey-bearded, a Custodian's sigil worn smooth by his thumb; eyes that measure distance
**Want:** to keep Dawnwatch standing long enough for the Nexus beneath it to stabilise
**Need / wound:** forgiveness for a community he once judged "could not be saved" and abandoned
**Secret** (table prep, not book canon): he knows more about the Mark of Balance than he says, and he has seen it before, on someone
who did not survive it. (Clue link: prep/session-02 §4 #7)
**Won't cross:** lie outright to someone he has taken responsibility for (Continuity Rule 10)
**Will cross under pressure:** withhold the truth, sacrifice himself, or sacrifice a district to save the city

## Voice
- **Diction:** formal, measured, deep; long sentences that end on a short one. Vocabulary of stone,
  watches and lines.
- **Verbal habit:** answers a hard question with a smaller true thing first, then the hard part.
- **Sample lines:**
  - "You carry the Mark. That is not a gift. It is a summons."
  - "The sword is but an extension of the heart. Mind which one you sharpen."
  - "I won't let the line die here." (reserved: say it once in Arc II, and again only at the worst moment)
- **Under pressure:** stands his ground; voice gets quieter and slower, never louder.

## Presence
- Physical habit: touches the worn sigil before he answers.
- Sensory signature: the **Nexus hum**, felt in the teeth, when he is near the obsidian tower.

## Relationships
- To Rikan: sees a disruptor who will cost the world something; chooses to train him anyway.
- To the Custodians: an Interventionist among Containment Absolutists; tolerated, watched.
- To Elara: a shared history going back decades (Book 1); she trusts him when she trusts no one.

## Arc Options
1. If Rikan trusts him fully, Alaric becomes the father figure he never had, and his loss (album
   T15) lands as grief.
2. If Rikan keeps the Shard secret, Alaric discovers it himself and their trust fractures.
3. If the PCs break the siege front early, Alaric survives Arc III and faces the Custodian schism instead.
