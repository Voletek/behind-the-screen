# The Shadow Weaver
<!-- npc: shadow-weaver · tier: pillar (villain) · campaign: fractured-shadows -->

**Function:** the hidden mastermind; dread before revelation (album T13)
**Look:** never shown before Arc III (Continuity Rule 11). Only effects: frost-black threads on doors,
Riftborn moving with purpose, a name whispered in many voices.
**Want:** to reach the Nexus beneath Dawnwatch and re-weave Balance to her own pattern
**Need / wound:** (author to decide; keep it human-sized when revealed)
**Secret:** she has been steering the Riftborn for years (Book 1), and she is not the top of the
structure: the Obsidian Covenant and "the Architect" have their own plans for her (Book 2).
**Won't cross:** (author to decide)
**Will cross under pressure:** anything, including her own servants

## Voice (from Arc III on)
- **Diction:** soft, intimate, patient; speaks as if she has already won.
- **Verbal habit:** names people's fears back to them in their own words.
- **Before Arc III:** she has no voice. Her presence is the choir-whisper of her name and silence
  where there should be sound.

## Presence
- Sensory signature: **sudden silence.** Insects, wind and dripping water stop.

## Front
`dawnwatch-siege` clock (bible §8). Advance it between sessions when the PCs aren't opposing her plan.

## Arc Options
1. The PCs uncover her through Malkor's notes (Book 1 path).
2. The PCs find her through Skrit, who has seen what moves in the deep Mire.
3. The PCs never confront her directly in Arc III, because they hold the gate and she withdraws, angrier.
