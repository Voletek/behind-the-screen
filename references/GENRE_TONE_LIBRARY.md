# Genre and Tone Library

> The GM equivalent of songwriting-kb's style library: ready-made tone packages for session zero
> (`core/methodology/session-zero.md` Step 3). Each package lists what the GM narrates, what the
> world rewards, and the AI drift to watch.

---

## How to Use

1. Pick a package (or two at 70/30: one dominant, one flavour).
2. Copy the **Tone line** and **Touchstone types** into `BIBLE.md → ## Tone`.
3. Put the **Drift watch** into table preferences.

---

## Packages

### Heroic Fantasy
- **Tone line:** brave people against real dangers; victory is possible and costs something.
- **Narrate:** wide vistas, named swords, taverns with history, monsters with lairs.
- **World rewards:** courage, teamwork, clever plans.
- **Lethality:** moderate. **Humour:** light banter.
- **Drift watch:** power creep, everything becoming epic.
- **Fits:** `dnd-5e`, `rules-light`.

### Grim / Dark Fantasy
- **Tone line:** a broken world where every power has a price and survival is a victory.
- **Narrate:** scarcity, cost, mud and blood, moral grey.
- **World rewards:** caution, sacrifice, hard choices.
- **Lethality:** high (agreed). **Humour:** gallows.
- **Drift watch:** tone softening into heroic; cruelty without meaning.
- **Fits:** `dnd-5e` with house rules, `rules-light` with Strain. Example: Fractured Shadows.

### Folk Horror
- **Tone line:** the old ways are true, the village knows, and the land wants something.
- **Narrate:** rituals, weather, songs, the wrongness of ordinary things.
- **World rewards:** paying attention, respecting rules, leaving in time.
- **Lethality:** high but slow. **Humour:** rare, nervous.
- **Drift watch:** jump scares instead of dread; explaining the horror.
- **Fits:** `dnd-5e`, `rules-light`. Example: Keeper of the Light.

### Cosmic Horror
- **Tone line:** knowledge costs sanity; humanity is small.
- **Narrate:** scale, impossibility, sensory wrongness, partial glimpses.
- **World rewards:** curiosity, at a price.
- **Drift watch:** showing the monster fully.

### Pastoral Peril (Mouse Guard)
- **Tone line:** small, earnest heroes in a huge, indifferent world; duty matters.
- **Narrate:** weather, seasons, scale (a stream is a river), craft and community.
- **World rewards:** duty, preparation, sacrifice for others.
- **Lethality:** real, especially from predators. **Humour:** gentle.
- **Drift watch:** cuteness; d20 logic.
- **Fits:** `mouse-guard`.

### Woodland Intrigue (Root)
- **Tone line:** storybook animals, real politics; every job helps someone and hurts someone else.
- **Narrate:** clearings, faction banners, markets, the war's cost on ordinary denizens.
- **World rewards:** cunning, reputation management, choosing sides (or not).
- **Drift watch:** factions becoming good vs evil; forgetting reputation.
- **Fits:** `root`.

### Street-Level Superheroes
- **Tone line:** powers don't fix everything; the neighbourhood comes first.
- **Narrate:** rooftops, rain, civilians, secret identities, rent.
- **World rewards:** protecting people, keeping promises.
- **Drift watch:** cosmic escalation; canon heroes stealing scenes.
- **Fits:** `marvel-multiverse`.

### Cosmic Superheroes
- **Tone line:** the fate of worlds, told with heart.
- **Narrate:** spectacle, scale, sacrifice, team dynamics.
- **Fits:** `marvel-multiverse` (high rank).

### Gritty Comic Mutants (TMNT original-comic feel)
- **Tone line:** outsiders in the sewers doing violent, necessary work in a world that fears them.
- **Narrate:** grime, neon, ninja clans, weird science, family loyalty.
- **Lethality:** high. **Humour:** dry, brotherly.
- **Fits:** `tmnt`, `rules-light`.

### Saturday-Morning Mutants
- **Tone line:** pizza, puns and pals; villains have silly plans and come back next week.
- **Narrate:** colour, catchphrases, gadgets, vehicles.
- **Lethality:** none (defeat = captured, embarrassed). **Humour:** high.
- **Fits:** `rules-light`, `tmnt` with house rules. Good for kids.

### Mystery / Investigation
- **Tone line:** the truth is out there and fair; clever people can find it.
- **Narrate:** details, contradictions, witnesses with agendas.
- **World rewards:** deduction, persistence.
- **Drift watch:** gating clues behind rolls (Three Clue Rule).

### Heist
- **Tone line:** professionals, a target, a plan, and everything going wrong in style.
- **Narrate:** security layers, the crew's specialties, the clock.
- **Drift watch:** hard-countering the plan; railroading to the "real" way in.

### Political Intrigue
- **Tone line:** everyone wants something; words are weapons.
- **Narrate:** factions, favours, secrets, public faces vs private deals.
- **Drift watch:** NPCs who tell the truth too easily.

---

## Touchstone Types

Ask for 2-3 of: a novel, a film or series, a video game, a comic, an album. Albums work well as tone
anchors (Fractured Shadows and Keeper of the Light are concept albums before they are campaigns).
