# Session Review Reference — Score Anchors

> Score anchors for the 12 + 5 categories in `core/methodology/session-review.md`. Use them to
> keep scores consistent across reviews. Report format: `SESSION_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md`.

---

## Layer 1 — Craft Categories

For each category: what a 9-10, a 6-7 and a 3-4 look like.

| # | Category | 9-10 | 6-7 | 3-4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Player Agency | Players changed the session's direction; consequences visible and remembered | Choices mattered locally; some steering | Outcomes fixed; choices cosmetic |
| 2 | Pacing | Scenes start late, end early; clear tension/release rhythm | One or two scenes dragged | Long stretches with no question at stake |
| 3 | Scene Framing | Vivid, short, ends on a hook every time | Mostly clear; some walls of text | Confusing, long, no clear thing to act on |
| 4 | Adjudication | Rolls only when needed; stakes clear; failures fork the story | Some unnecessary rolls or flat failures | Rolls for everything; "nothing happens" |
| 5 | Rules Fidelity | Rules right or rulings labelled and logged | Minor errors, quickly fixed | Wrong system logic; invented rules stated as RAW |
| 6 | Dice Honesty | Every roll sourced and logged per table settings | A few unlogged minor rolls | Fabricated rolls (cap 2) |
| 7 | NPC Voice | NPCs distinct, brief, consistent with sheets | Distinct but chatty | NPCs interchangeable or monologuing |
| 8 | World Consistency | No contradictions; off-screen world moved | Small slips, fixed | Contradictions with state/bible |
| 9 | Spotlight Balance | Spotlight within ±30% across players; every PC goal touched | One player under-served | One player ran the session |
| 10 | Tone Fidelity | Every scene felt like the touchstones | Occasional drift | Wrong genre feel |
| 11 | Safety & Consent | Tools honoured instantly; check-ins at the right moments | Tools honoured, a bit clumsily | Line crossed or tool ignored (cap 1; composite cap 5.0) |
| 12 | Fun & Momentum | Players asked for the next session | Enjoyable but forgettable | Players disengaged |

## Layer 2 — AI-GM Assessments

| # | Assessment | 9-10 | 6-7 | 3-4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | No Puppeting | Zero instances | 1-2 slips | Repeated narration of PC choices/feelings |
| A2 | Concision | Messages matched the table setting; no unrequested menus | Some long blocks or menus | Walls of text throughout |
| A3 | Anti-Sycophancy | Real failures with teeth; NPCs pushed back | Mostly fair; a few freebies | Everything worked |
| A4 | State Discipline | State written as it changed; files alone can restart play | Written at the end only | State missing or wrong |
| A5 | Prep Use | Flexible use; skipped content recycled; clues delivered | Some railroading | Prep forced onto players |

## Evidence Rules

- Quote the transcript (≤ 2 lines) or cite a dice-log line for every score below 7.
- Count, don't estimate: number of puppeting instances, average message length, spotlight counts.
- Table settings override defaults: a "lush narration" table isn't penalised for long descriptions
  unless they buried the hook.

## Calibration

- A competent, enjoyable session usually scores 7-8. Reserve 9-10 for sessions that would be a good
  example for other GMs.
- Score the GM, never the players.
- Re-reviews: report the delta per category and whether last review's top 3 recommendations were applied.
