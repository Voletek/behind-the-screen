# NPC Voice Reference

> Data for `core/methodology/npc-voice.md`. The text equivalent of songwriting-kb's character-voice
> reference: diction, verbal habits, light-touch dialect, and the voice sheet template.

---

## Diction Axes

Pick one position on each axis for a pillar NPC. Contrast NPCs who share scenes on at least two axes.

| Axis | One end | Other end |
|---|---|---|
| Register | Formal ("I would advise against it.") | Casual ("Bad idea, mate.") |
| Sentence length | Clipped ("No. Tomorrow. Go.") | Rolling ("Well now, if you'd asked me last spring, before the river turned…") |
| Directness | Blunt | Evasive / answers with questions |
| Vocabulary field | Trade (sailor, smith, scholar, soldier, farmer, priest, thief) | — |
| Emotional temperature | Warm | Cold |
| Certainty | Absolute | Hedging ("perhaps", "they say") |
| Era feel | Archaic ("thee", "aye") | Modern |

## Verbal Habits (pick one)

| Habit | Example |
|---|---|
| Address term | Calls everyone "little one" / "friend" / by surname |
| Catchphrase | "Stone remembers." |
| Counting | Lists things in threes, every time |
| Proverbs | Speaks in sayings from their culture |
| Questions | Answers questions with questions |
| Third person | Refers to self by name or title |
| Food/weather metaphors | "That plan's half-baked." |
| Precise numbers | "Forty-one steps to the gate. I counted." |
| Unfinished sentences | Trails off when the topic gets close to their secret |
| Formal titles | Never drops "Captain", "Custodian", "Ser" |
| Laughs at wrong moments | Nervous habit, shows fear |
| Swears by something | "By the Nexus." "Shade and water." |

## Light-Touch Dialect

Use 2-4 marker words or grammar features, not phonetic spelling of everything. Respectful, never a
caricature of a real-world group (CONTRIBUTING.md).

| Flavour | Markers |
|---|---|
| Northern / Scots-like | "aye", "wee", "ken" (know), "lass/lad", "dinnae" (sparingly) |
| Sailor | Nautical vocabulary over pirate clichés: "below decks", "lee side", "can't fathom it", "make fast" |
| Old formal | "thee/thou" for intimates only, "ere", "mayhap" |
| Freehold (Eryndor) | "bro/sis/cuz" as obligation, not slang (see Fractured Shadows bible) |
| Ashen Sands (Eryndor) | Clipped survivor cadence; blessings of shade and water; insults about dry graves |
| Street / modern | Contractions, slang of the setting's own invention |
| Scholar | Subordinate clauses, citations ("as Valen wrote…") |

For non-English-flavoured accents, prefer syntax and idiom over spelling changes.

## Voice Under Pressure

| Emotion | Common voice shift |
|---|---|
| Fear | Shorter sentences, more questions, formal politeness (or swearing) |
| Anger | Clipped, louder, drops titles — or very quiet and precise (more frightening) |
| Grief | Repeats a name or phrase; long pauses (written as "…" or a described silence) |
| Lying | Over-detailed, or suspiciously short; verbal habit disappears |
| Joy | Habit amplified; talks faster |

## Non-Verbal Presence (one per NPC)

Cleans spectacles · never sits with back to the door · hums one tune · smells of tar/ink/smoke ·
taps a ring · always eating · tail flicks (Ratfolk) · stands too close · avoids eye contact ·
scar they touch when lying.
