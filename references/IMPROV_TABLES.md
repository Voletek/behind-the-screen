> (`core/methodology/running-the-game.md` → Improvisation). Record whatever you use (Tenet 6).
> All entries are original to this repo and setting-neutral; rename to fit your world.

## Complications (1d12)

1. Someone recognises a PC, wrongly.
2. The weather turns, hard.
3. A rival group wants the same thing, now.
4. The contact is dead/missing/replaced.
5. The price just doubled.
6. A child (or small creature) is in the way, in danger, or watching.
7. The ground/structure gives way.
8. An old promise is called in.
9. A faction's soldiers arrive for unrelated reasons.
10. The item is a fake (or the real one is cursed).
11. Fire.
12. Exactly what they wanted, plus someone they didn't want to meet.

## Rumours (1d12)

1. "The well water tastes of iron since the lights in the hills."
2. "The baron's heir hasn't been seen at mass in a month."
3. "Someone pays silver for black feathers."
4. "The old road is open again. Nobody who took it came back to say."
5. "The ferryman knows every face that crosses. Every one."
6. "Two caravans, same night, same pass, same silence."
7. "The bells rang themselves at midnight."
8. "The guard captain owes the wrong people."
9. "A healer in the market cures anything, for a memory."
10. "The mine reopened. The miners sing in the dark now."
11. "The prophet wasn't lying. That's the problem."
12. True, about the PCs, and spreading.

## Quick Locations (1d12)

1. A tavern with one locked door nobody talks about.
2. A bridge with a toll and a troll-shaped gap in the rules.
3. A market built in a drained reservoir.
4. A shrine whose offerings move overnight.
5. A burned farmstead with fresh footprints.
6. A tower with no door at ground level.
7. A cave that breathes warm air.
8. An archive where the shelves rearrange.
9. A ferry crossing, the ferry on the wrong bank.
10. A battlefield turned orchard.
11. A guildhall mid-election.
12. A camp of refugees who all tell the same story.

## NPC Reactions (2d6)

| 2d6 | Starting attitude |
|---|---|
| 2-3 | Hostile |
| 4-5 | Unfriendly / wary |
| 6-8 | Neutral / indifferent |
| 9-10 | Friendly |
| 11-12 | Helpful |

Adjust by reputation and approach. Record the result as `disposition` in `state.json`.

## Loot Flavour (1d12)

1. Map with one place circled and one crossed out. 2. Letter in a code. 3. A key, warm.
4. Coins from a kingdom that no longer exists. 5. A tooth on a string. 6. A bottle of something
that glows faintly. 7. A small portrait of a PC. 8. A whistle that makes no sound humans hear.
9. Fine boots, one size too small. 10. A ledger of debts. 11. A seed that hums. 12. A badge of
office, bloodstained.
