# Safety Tools Reference

> Supports Tenet 1 (Safety and Consent First) and `core/methodology/session-zero.md` Step 4.
> Sources: TTRPG Safety Toolkit (Kienna Shaw & Lauren Bryant-Monk) [Tier 2]; lines and veils
> (Ron Edwards, *Sex and Sorcery*, 2003) [Tier 2]; X-Card (John Stavropoulos) [Tier 2];
> Script Change (Beau Jágr Sheldon) [Tier 2]. Paraphrased; see the originals for full text.

## Core Tools

| Tool | When | How it works in text chat |
|---|---|---|
| **Lines** | Before play | Content that never appears in the game, even off-screen |
| **Veils** | Before play | Content that may happen, but off-screen ("fade to black") |
| **X-Card** | During play | A player types `X` (or `/x`) alone on a line. The GM removes the last content, no explanation asked, and moves on |
| **Pause / Rewind / Fast-forward** (Script Change) | During play | `/pause` (break, check in), `/rewind` (redo the last bit differently), `/ff` (skip ahead past this) |
| **Open door** | Always | Anyone may leave or step away at any time, no questions |
| **Check-in** | After intense scenes; when a player goes quiet | GM asks OOC: "Quick check: everyone good to continue in this direction?" |
| **Stars and wishes** | End of session | Each player names a favourite moment and something they'd like to see |

## How the AI GM Responds to a Safety Tool

1. **Stop immediately.** Do not finish the description.
2. **Acknowledge briefly, OOC:** "Got it, removing that."
3. **Remove or rewind** the content. Offer a neutral continuation.
4. **Never ask why**, never argue, never make the player justify it.
5. **Record** the new line or veil in `BIBLE.md → ## Safety` if the player wants it to persist (ask
   once, privately if possible).
6. **Don't over-apologise** or make it a big moment; that puts the player on the spot.

## Default Lines (this repo applies them unless the table explicitly opts in)

- Sexual content involving minors (absolute; no opt-in exists).
- Sexual violence (absolute line by default).
- Real-world hate speech and slurs directed at real groups.
- Graphic torture of player characters.
- Real people as targets.

## Common Veils (ask)

- Sexual content between adults (default: veil)
- Graphic gore
- Harm to children or animals
- Self-harm and suicide
- Body horror
- Addiction
- Phobias (spiders, drowning, enclosed spaces, insects)

## Lines & Veils Questionnaire (session zero)

Ask each player, in a private message if the platform allows:

```
Anything that should NEVER appear (lines)?
Anything that may happen but off-screen (veils)?
Any phobias or personal topics to avoid?
How should I check in with you (OOC message, a quick "you good?", nothing unless you signal)?
```

Record answers without names in a shared bible if players prefer anonymity.

## Horror and Dark Campaigns

Dark content is fine when the table opts in. For horror (e.g. *Keeper of the Light*):
- Agree on the **kind** of horror: dread, body horror, cosmic, psychological, gore.
- Use **escalation with consent**: name the horror tier you'll reach by the end.
- Check in after the first truly frightening scene.
- Prefer implication over explicit gore (also better horror craft).

## Group Play Notes

- In a shared chat, any player's `X` applies to the whole table.
- If a player keeps pushing content another player has lined, the GM pauses play OOC and restates the
  agreement. The line wins (Tenet 1).

## Minors at the Table

If any player is a minor, all default veils become lines, romance is off, and violence is described
without gore. Record it in the bible.
